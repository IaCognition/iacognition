# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the IaCognition community a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or professional role.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a productive community include:

- discussing technical disagreements with evidence and respect;
- acknowledging tradeoffs and uncertainty;
- giving and accepting constructive review;
- focusing criticism on ideas, designs, and code rather than people;
- respecting differing infrastructure constraints and operating environments.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, or publication of another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate them.

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainers.
